Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Choo-Choo Fun has no accounts or sign-in. There is no profile to set up or account to delete.

Where are best times saved?

Best completion times are saved locally on your device, separately for each game and difficulty. They are not sent to MBChooChoo, LLC. Device backups may include this data depending on your Apple settings.

How do I reset best times?

On the app’s home screen, turn on timer mode, then tap Reset in the Best Times board. This removes the saved best times for all games and difficulties on that device.

What can I try if I cannot hear the audio?

Turn up your device’s media volume and check whether audio is playing through connected Bluetooth headphones or speakers. Close and reopen the app if needed. The app uses included recordings and may fall back to Apple’s built-in speech synthesis.

Does the app record my child’s voice?

No. Choo-Choo Fun plays audio; it does not record audio or request microphone access. It also does not request camera, photo library, contacts, or location access.

How do I remove local app data?

Reset best times within the app, or delete the app to remove its local records from that device. Offloading the app preserves its data. Manage any backup copies through your Apple backup settings.

Are there advertisements or analytics?

The current app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection services. See the privacy policy for details about the app, support email, and this website.
