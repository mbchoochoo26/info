Choo-Choo Fun Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 11, 2026

The Choo-Choo Fun app does not collect personal information. Best-time records stay on your device.

About this policy

MBChooChoo, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company, provides Choo-Choo Fun. This policy describes privacy in the iPhone and iPad app, including its word, number, picture, and clock activities. It also explains what happens when an adult contacts us for support or visits this website.

Information collected by the app

Choo-Choo Fun does not collect or transmit personal information to MBChooChoo, LLC. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection services. We do not sell or share personal information collected through the app, because the app collects none.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the best completion time for each game and difficulty on your device so you can see previous records. These records do not include names or other profile information and are not sent to us. They remain until you reset them or delete the app. Your device’s backup services may include this local app data, depending on your Apple settings.

Removing best times and local data

To remove all saved best times, turn on timer mode on the home screen and tap Reset in the Best Times board. Deleting the app also removes its local records from that device. Offloading the app preserves app data. Copies in device backups are managed through your Apple backup settings.

Children’s privacy

Choo-Choo Fun is designed for children and does not ask children for names, email addresses, birth dates, location, photos, or other personal information. There is no chat, user content upload, or social sharing feature. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Support correspondence should be sent by a parent or caregiver without including a child’s personal information.

Audio, pictures, and device access

Learning recordings and pictures are included with the app. The app plays audio and may use Apple’s built-in speech synthesis if a recording cannot play. It does not record your voice or request microphone, camera, photo library, contacts, or location access.

Apple services

Apple manages App Store downloads, purchases of the app if applicable, and any device backups you enable. Apple may process information under its own privacy policy and your device settings. We do not receive your payment details from the app.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and handle follow-up. Our email service provider processes these messages to deliver and store email. We retain correspondence as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or as required by law. You can request deletion by emailing us at the address below. Please do not send children’s personal information.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, cookies, or contact forms. GitHub may collect technical information, including visitor IP addresses, to operate and secure its services under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This hosting activity is separate from the Choo-Choo Fun app, which does not transmit personal information to us.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Any future app data collection will be described before it is introduced.
